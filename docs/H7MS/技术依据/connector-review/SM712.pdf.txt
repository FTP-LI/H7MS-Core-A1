General Information
The CDSOT23-SM712 device provides ESD, EFT and Surge 
protection for data ports meeting IEC 61000-4-2 (ESD), IEC 61000-
4-4 (EFT) and IEC 61000-4-5 (Surge) requirements. The Transient 
Voltage Suppressor Array offers two TVS diodes with a Working 
Peak Reverse Voltage of 7 V or 12 V and Minimum Breakdown 
Voltage of 7.5 V or 13.3 V respectively.
The SOT23 packaged device will mount directly onto the industry 
standard SOT23 footprint. Bourns® Chip Diodes conform to 
JEDEC standards, are easy to handle with standard pick and place 
equipment and the flat configuration minimizes roll away.
